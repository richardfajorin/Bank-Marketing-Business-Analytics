# Bank Marketing Business Analytics

## Understanding Term Deposit Subscription Behavior

**Richard Fajorin**
AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship, Week 5

### Overview

This project analyzes the Bank Marketing dataset to understand which customer characteristics and campaign conditions are associated with term deposit subscription.

The analysis moves from data cleaning and exploratory analysis to business insights and recommendations.

### Business Problem

Why do some clients subscribe to a term deposit while others do not, and which customer segments are more likely to respond positively to marketing campaigns?

### Dataset

* **11,162 client records**
* Target variable: `deposit`
* Numeric features include age, balance, duration, campaign history, and previous contacts.
* Categorical features include job, education, marital status, contact method, month, and previous campaign outcome.

### Analysis

The project covers:

* Data cleaning and quality checks
* Exploratory data analysis
* Customer segmentation
* Subscription-rate analysis
* Campaign performance analysis
* Contact-method analysis
* Monthly campaign analysis
* Call-duration analysis
* Business recommendations

### Key Findings

* Overall subscription rate: **47.4%**
* Clients with a previous successful campaign outcome had a **91.3%** subscription rate.
* Students and retirees were among the highest-converting job groups.
* Tertiary-educated clients had a higher subscription rate than primary-educated clients.
* Subscription rates varied considerably by contact method and month.
* Subscribers generally had substantially longer calls than non-subscribers.
